MODULE 04

Security Notes

LEGACY ALGORITHM

Why DES is weak today

DES uses a 56-bit effective key, giving 2⁵⁶ possible keys. Exhaustive search becomes feasible for sufficiently capable attackers, so DES is not appropriate for protecting modern sensitive information.

What this experiment proves

The app measures a real search over a deliberately small key-index space. It demonstrates attempts and timing, but it does not directly measure the time needed to search all 2⁵⁶ DES keys.

Important limitations

DES-CBC here is for education only. CBC does not provide integrity by itself. For modern applications, use a vetted authenticated-encryption scheme such as AES-GCM through a maintained cryptographic library.
